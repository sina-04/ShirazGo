# Line 1 timetable

## How to read this timetable

- All times use the **24-hour clock** and local Shiraz time.
- **Working day** and **Weekend / holiday** follow the service categories printed in the supplied timetable.
- Trains are scheduled at **15-minute intervals**.
- Use the direction heading that matches your destination: **Toward Ehsan** or **Toward Shahid Dastgheyb**.
- In each detailed table, the hour is shown in the first column and the departure minutes are shown in the second. Example: `06: 10, 25, 40, 55` means 06:10, 06:25, 06:40, and 06:55.
- Operational changes may occur. Arrive a few minutes early and check station notices.
